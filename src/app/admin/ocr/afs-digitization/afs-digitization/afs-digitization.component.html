AFS Digitization

Upload a scanned ULB financial-statement PDF. Amazon Textract extracts tables and key/value pairs into an Excel workbook (confidence score), then Gemini re-reads the source PDF and cross-checks every extracted value (accuracy score). Optionally, Gemini also checks the arithmetic of the digitized output — totals tally, assets equal liabilities, and sign/zero/numeric rules (arithmetic assessment) — and identifies each document in the PDF with its page range, flagging merged or duplicate documents (detected document type).
