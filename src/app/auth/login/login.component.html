The XVI-FC urban grant, in sixty seconds

Every five years the Finance Commission decides how central tax revenue is shared with states and local governments. The Sixteenth Commission's award covers FY 2026–31 and earmarks grants for urban local bodies — tied to conditions on accounts, property tax and service delivery.

cityfinance.in is where those conditions are evidenced and those grants are administered. If you run a municipality, this is where your claim lives. If you study Indian cities, this is where the numbers are.
